Extending DaisyRec for Item Ranking
1. Introduction
This report represents the implementation and evaluation of the SimpleX model as a new recommender algorithm integrated into the DaisyRec library without altering the daisy code for the time ranking. The baseline models selected in this task are Matrix Factorization (MF) and Neural Matrix Factorization (NeuMF) for comparison purposes.
The SimpleX algorithm uses Cosine Contrastive Loss (CCL) with a margin and negative margin.
The two datasets chosen for this project are MovieLens 1M and Yelp dataset. These datasets are quite large and when trained on the algorithms, the computational power is quite large. To fasten the process, the data was sampled to only 50,000 interactions to ensure smooth training on limited hardware representation.

Analysis
In the Yelp-200k dataset shown in the Table 4 above, SimpleX algorithm significantly outperforms the two baseline models; MF and NeuMF, as shown by accuracy matrices. The SimpleX recommends a wide range of items as shown by the Coverage matrix (0.8529) and exposure is fair as shown by both gini (0.4854) and entropy (12.7929). The high wide coverage and relative exposure indicates that simplex generalizes sparse review data well due to SimpleX’s cosine contrastive loss and use of user history data embeddings.
The three algorithms show a relatively balanced performance for the MovieLens dataset. SimpleX algorithm is outperformed by baseline models in accuracy matrices. However, SimpleX shows slightly wide coverage of 0.6548, NeuMF (0.4254) and MF (0.5339). In terms of exposure, the SimpleX was relatively better with a gini value of 0.6548 and entropy 9.8119.
In conclusion, SimpleX shows better coverage in both datasets, indicating that it is recommended for recommender systems. SimpleX also facilitates a better exposure as shown in both gini performances. Lastly, SimpleX performs well in the diversity recommendation as shown by the entropy performance.
